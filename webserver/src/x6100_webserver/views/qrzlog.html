QRZ Logbook

Every QSO the radio logs, in every mode (FT8, FT4, FT2, JS8, voice, CW, Field Ops activations), goes to your QRZ Logbook as well, a few seconds after it is logged; the radio's own logs stay as they are. Without a network the QSOs wait on the SD card and go when the radio is back online. The display says what happened: "QRZ: DL1ABC uploaded".

UploadTakes effect: at the next QSO

Up to three logbooks, a callsign with its Logbook API key (QRZ.com, Logbook, Settings). QRZ keeps one logbook per callsign, so KD0CQ/P is its own profile. A QSO goes to the profile with the callsign the radio logged it with. Switched on, the QSOs from that moment on are sent, not the log from before. Uploading needs a QRZ.com Logbook API key, which QRZ.com only gives with a paid QRZ.com subscription (XML level or higher). The same profiles are on Field Ops and on the radio, Settings > Logbook.
